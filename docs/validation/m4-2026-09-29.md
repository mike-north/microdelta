## Limits

- M4 proves the bounded composition runtime on one host with the single-writer
  SQLite History. It makes no M5 claim: no retries, quota waits, cancellation
  mechanics, concurrency or fencing. `cancelled` outcomes are typed but
  injected only through the admission port.
- Soundness is bounded by the tracked-influence contract (CX-1, CX-2); the
  tracked-captures lint guides it but does not sandbox JavaScript.
- Tolerant (outcome) folds are not implemented.
- Known follow-ups, none blocking the exit: #100 (supplied argument-list edge
  cases), #106 (bounding the wait for unsettled nested calls under run
  cancellation), and the pre-existing #77 (release receipts).
