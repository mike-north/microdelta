## Exit criteria

The M5 exit has six parts:

- A-09 through A-14, the A-19 middleware and fault cases, and A-18 lifecycle isolation.
- Deterministic interleavings and fresh processes prove that a stale worker cannot publish, renew or release for another holder.
- Known usage survives faults exactly once.
- Unknown work is never reported as free.
- Presentation failure cannot cause re-execution of committed success.
- There is no real paid acceptance.

## Limits

- **One writer.** There is one fenced writer per store. M5 makes no claim of
  multi-writer parallelism; that is deferred to M7 measurement. Lease waiting is not
  proven fair, and eventual grant is unproven. The operator deadline bounds a wait.
- **Single host, fault model.** Evidence is single-host SQLite with process kills.
  It makes no power-loss, filesystem, network-filesystem or multi-host claim.
- **No real providers.** There are no real paid providers and no spend. The
  paid-like assessor and the providers in the tests are deterministic fakes.
- **Accounting is private and injected.** `@microdelta/accounting` stays
  `private: true`, and the facade receives Accounting through Supervision's
  structural port. Registering the package for publishing is a pending owner
  decision, after which the facade may build the default adapter itself
  ([#151](https://github.com/mike-north/microdelta/issues/151)).
- **Re-paying on resume.** A step body that makes raw paid calls pays again on
  resume after a kill before publication (EXP-8 CX-3). Isolating each paid call in
  a memoized child step avoids this; the acceptance suite asserts both.
- **No value on operator resolution.** Operator resolution carries no result value.
  A resolved-succeeded operation fails its member with `operation-resolved` until an
  operator abandons it. Result-carrying resolution is M6 work
  ([#152](https://github.com/mike-north/microdelta/issues/152)).
- **Open follow-ups**, none of them gating the exit:
  - #129: History REPLACE hardening.
  - #138: typed busy for History data operations.
  - #144: five Resolution fault sites without a rejecting test.
  - #145: boundary lint for dynamic `import()`.
  - #151: the facade constructs Accounting's durable adapter once the package is registered.
  - #152: operator resolution that carries a result value (M6 operator surface).
  - Pre-existing and out of scope: #77, #100.

## #140 gate disposition

[#140](https://github.com/mike-north/microdelta/issues/140) gated the M5 decision
(root's comments on [#123](https://github.com/mike-north/microdelta/issues/123#issuecomment-5934430158)
and #149). It is now disposed of by
[PR #150](https://github.com/mike-north/microdelta/pull/150), merged as `eab6393`. The
full evidence is in the [concurrency record's #140 addendum](m5-concurrency-2026-09-30.md#addendum-c3s-delayed-grant-made-deterministic-2026-10-07-issue-140).

### Cause

On `main` at `7800756`,
[`Check` run 36815834725](https://github.com/mike-north/microdelta/actions/runs/36815834725)
failed in its Node 24 job. In C3, the real-timer contention case, the assertion
`grants.some(grant => grant.tries > 1)` found no grant after a refused attempt.
Every earlier C3 assertion on that run passed, so nothing points to a fencing or
busy defect.

The test's free-running schedule alone produces this failure. A waiter gives up 60 ms
after its first try, so on a slow host no grant ever follows a refused attempt. The
failure was reproduced under load, not re-observed in CI.

An independent reproduction at `3df7c57`:

| Profile | Failed | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 12 parallel runs + 48 CPU burners | 4 of 24 | 2 at `tries > 1`, 2 at `busy > 0` |
| 16 parallel runs + 72 CPU burners | 10 of 32 | all at `tries > 1` |

### Repair

C3 now begins with a parent-coordinated forced handover:

1. Q0 holds the lease.
2. Q1's first try is refused, naming Q0.
3. Q2 reaches its writer-busy deadline, naming Q0.
4. Only then does Q0 release.
5. Q1 is granted the next fence on a later try, before Q0's lease would have expired.

Each step is polled through a read-only `wait-status` command and bounded, with
diagnostics that report the worker's full status if a step is missed. The
`tries > 1` assertion is preserved unchanged.

The free-running phase keeps host-independent floors: every worker runs at least one
tenure, and at least one free-running grant occurs. It is no longer required to show
a delayed grant or a deadline outcome. The concurrency record's mapping rows that cited C3 now rest
on the forced phase, and the rows that wrongly cited C3 for expiry are corrected.
Expiry is covered by L1, L2, L4, W4 and `writer-wait.test.ts`.
